Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Nether AG, Bern, Switzerland, is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. This policy covers our website, correspondence, and applications for roles at Nether AG.

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@nether.ch.

2. Visiting our website

Our website uses an external hosting provider and Cloudflare for delivery and security. These providers process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, requested pages, and access times to deliver and protect the website.

We load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to download them, sending technical information including your IP address.

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies on this website. Cloudflare may use security cookies to distinguish legitimate visitors from automated or malicious traffic.

For more information about Cloudflare and Google, see the privacy policies of Cloudflare and Google.

3. Contact and applications

If you contact us or apply for a role, we process the information you send to respond to your enquiry or assess your application. This may include your name, email address, portfolio, availability, and other information you choose to share.

We use an external email service provider to send, receive, and store correspondence. This provider processes messages, attachments, and associated technical information for these purposes.

4. Retention and processing abroad

We retain correspondence and application information for as long as needed to handle the enquiry or recruitment process, or to meet applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes. We then delete information that is no longer needed.

Our service providers may process data outside Switzerland, including in the United States. Their privacy policies describe their international processing and safeguards. Technical logs and service data are subject to the relevant providers’ retention practices.

5. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you may request access to your personal data and ask for correction or deletion. Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it. Contact us using the email address above; legal exceptions or retention obligations may apply.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The date below shows the latest revision.
